# Responsible use

Mirage exists because swapping faces with a friend in the middle of a team
call is funny. It stays funny only as long as everyone is in on the joke. So,
a few ground rules. They are short, and we mean them.

## Consent first

- **Your face is yours; other faces are not.** Only use a real person's face if
  they said yes, and they know roughly how you are going to use it.
- **Generated faces** (the 🎲 button) do not belong to anyone, which makes them
  the easy, worry-free choice.
- **Public figures are people too.** Being famous is not consent.

## Never use Mirage to

- **Deceive or defraud**: fake identity checks, scams, "the boss asked me to
  wire the money" calls.
- **Impersonate someone for gain**: to get access, money, trust or an
  advantage you would not get as yourself.
- **Harass, bully or embarrass** anyone.
- **Make sexual content** with anyone's face. Ever.

If you are not sure whether something is okay, it probably is not.

## Tell people it's a joke

When you show up in a call as someone else, say so, early, before anyone
makes a decision based on who they think you are. A good prank ends with
everyone laughing, including the person whose face you borrowed.

## Laws and platform rules

Deepfake, likeness and privacy laws differ a lot between countries and are
changing fast. Some places require labelling synthetic media; some forbid
certain uses outright. Zoom, Google Meet and other platforms also have their
own rules. Following them is on you.

Mirage is also **personal, non-commercial** software: the face models it uses
are licensed for non-commercial use only (see [NOTICE.md](../NOTICE.md)).

## What Mirage does and does not do

**It runs 100% locally.** Your camera feed and your face library stay on your
Mac. Nothing is uploaded; there is no account and no analytics. The only
network use is downloading models (during install, the first time you choose
**Best**, or later if one is missing) and fetching a generated face when you
press 🎲.

## If something goes wrong

If you think someone used Mirage to hurt you or others, keep evidence and
contact the platform where it happened and, if needed, the police. If you find
a way Mirage makes abuse easier, please
[open an issue](https://github.com/RebSem/mirage/issues) so we can look at it.

Have fun, and be the kind of person people are happy to lend their face to.
